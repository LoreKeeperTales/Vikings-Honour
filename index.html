Campaign & Lore

Campaign I — Exile and Return

Olav Haraldsson returns to Norway and seeks to establish Christianity. The player opposes him and is driven into exile. They choose a destination, found a settlement, recruit allies, build an army and eventually return to confront Olav.

Historical note: Olav Haraldsson's Norwegian kingship belongs to the early 11th century (traditionally 1015–1028), later than AD 700–900. The campaign follows his period, while the settlement's food-economy inspiration draws from earlier Viking Age evidence.
